U6d-bots: fair v2 bot teamwork, a moving Killer and tuned balance

Attempt 1 (Codex): remove worker caps, coordinate timed rescues and escapes,
and add anonymous predictive pursuit, Signal-driven patrol, terminal kicks and
exit guarding. Match phantom pacing, restore the fairness bounds, and gate the
GDD v2 balance bands on both seed sets.

Attempt 2 (Codex, then Claude Opus after a quota switch): the Killer always
has a destination. When all work was checked recently it sweeps the least
recently checked terminal (a recent Signal counts as older), roams far map
spots against small teams, and does not re-kick a terminal within its revisit
window. Survivors work out of sight of a still, camping Killer, keep their
terminal unless another is 15 points ahead, rescue before holding a powered
switch, and detour around a visible Killer on the way out. Survivors freeze
Silent while an unseen or distant Killer is near, flee only at Threat 3, calm
down faster, and only a team of five or more abandons the threatened terminal.
A stalled leading switch sends a second holder to another powered switch. The
phantom worker mix follows the new survivor pacing. Guard escape-rescue
timing against zero speed.

Attempt 3 (Claude Opus, Codex out of credits): make "the match keeps moving"
a tested invariant. Every balance sweep config, plus one idle human survivor
with six bots, must never go 150 s without progress. A survivor that made no progress for 25 s commits: only Threat 3 or a
Killer closing within 10 m stops it, and it works the target farthest from
the Killer and out of its sight. Running out of match time commits too. A
stalled switch duty moves to the switch farthest from the Killer, and idle
teammates are not counted on for rescues, switches or terminal slots. The
Killer searches the map cell by cell after 45 s without a blip, patrols open
exits instead of guarding them, and pays more attention late in the match.
The escape detour is cached for a second.

Attempt 3 follow-up (Claude Opus, Codex out of credits): drop the late-match
"desperate" mode, which stopped survivor bots from fleeing even Threat 3 or a
Killer closing within 10 m. Running out of match time still commits a bot, but
the Threat 3 and closing-Killer interrupts always apply. Two regression tests
cover a committed, time-short survivor breaking off for both.
The invariant now only reports, and no longer
asserts, time-ups with zero captures: a lone survivor that keeps evading and
working can lose the race against the clock without any freeze (user
decision).
