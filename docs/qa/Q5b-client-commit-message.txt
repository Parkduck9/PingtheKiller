Q5b-client: shutters and vault rails in the Unity client

- Props: ChasePropSetup (a sibling of WorldArtSetup; it never touches
  WorldArt) builds one prefab per WorldChaseProps box into Art/ChaseProps
  and the Resources/ChaseProps catalog. ChasePropsView places them at the
  Shared box centres for both roles. If the catalog does not match the
  boxes, it builds the same ChasePropShapes from primitives.
- Shutter looks follow the WorldState shutter bytes: Up, Down (a 1.2 m
  slatted panel that drops in 0.12 s) and Broken (wrecked: a bent half
  leaning on a post, halves on the ground, in the light Edge grey).
  Rails are 3 m x 1.0 m, with thicker light tubes and a vault-point marker.
- Prompts: Survivors get pull and vault; the Killer gets force and rail
  vault. Ranges are the server's, and a prompt only fills an empty slot.
  Action rows cover Pull and Vault; the Killer hold block covers Break and
  Vault (UI/ChasePropCopy).
- Poses: a procedural overlay from PlayerState.Chase, timed to the server
  durations: pull, a hop vault, a hammering break, and a stun stagger.
- Stun: a danger halo ring facing the camera over the drawn Killer model
  (a flat ring was edge-on and invisible), plus the Hovl circle particles
  unless Reduced motion is on. The Killer gets a 0.25 s FOV
  impulse and the `Stunned` effect line.
- Sounds: procedural, positional ShutterSlam, ShutterCrunch and VaultThud.
  None plays on the first snapshot.
- HUD_SPEC section 15. EditMode tests in ChasePropsTests. The
  SurvivorFeedback QA audit now lists ChasePropsLayer as a Killer-entry
  reader (Chase flags only).
- DevCapture: dev-args event shots chase-shutter-down/broken, chase-stun,
  chase-vault-own/seen and chase-prompt (Survivor reads stay in the guarded loop).
- ChasePropShot: editor captures into docs/qa/Q5b-screens.
