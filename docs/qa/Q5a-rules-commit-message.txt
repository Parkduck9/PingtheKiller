Q5a-rules a2: bots use shutters and rails on purpose, not by chance

QA finding F2: chased bots almost never pulled or vaulted. Attempt 1 only
reacted to a prop within 3 m straight ahead (rails: killer within 2.5 m), so
use depended on chase geometry. ChaseTactics now plans:
- a chased survivor (killer visible within 8 m) picks the nearest Up shutter,
  rail or Down shutter on its side whose approach point is within 9 m and
  that it reaches first (killer distance >= 1.15 x its own + 1 m, path detour
  <= 1.4x), runs there on the A* route, pulls the shutter behind itself once
  the killer is within 2.3 m of the box (or after 2 s), or vaults;
- the killer walks to a Down shutter within 6 m and breaks it when going
  around is more than 8 m longer than through, and vaults a rail after a
  target that has crossed it back and forth twice within 12 s.
A* vault-link costs: survivor 2 m, killer 6 m. Phantoms still never vault.
No rule number changed.

Also: a Dummy Packet cast mid-vault spawns its phantom on the nearer
standable end, not over the rail; BotMatchTests allows a mid-vault body over
a prop (never a wall); new ChasePropsBotUseTests floor (>= 1 pull and >= 1
rail vault per match, 12 seeded 1v7 all-bot matches, 300 s each) and
ChasePlanTests.
