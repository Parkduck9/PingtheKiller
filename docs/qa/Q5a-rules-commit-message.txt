Q5a-rules: shutters and vault rails on the server, with bot use

GDD v2 section 8. Ten roll-down shutters and eight low rails (WorldChaseProps)
block movement but never sight. A Normal survivor pulls an Up shutter within
1.5 m (0.3 s, frozen); a killer within the box + 0.6 m is stunned 2 s (no move,
capture, interact or skill) and pushed to the far side; bodies in the gap are
pushed out, the puller keeps its side. Survivors vault a Down shutter (1.0 s);
both roles vault rails (0.8 s / 1.6 s); the killer breaks a Down shutter by
holding still for 2 s (Broken is permanent). Pulls and vaults force Signal
for their length plus 1 s. Interact priority: rescue contact > props (fresh
press, nearest in range) > switch/terminal (killer: props > hatch > kick).

WorldState carries the shutter states for everyone; PlayerState.Chase flags go
to survivors (anonymous killer entries keep 0); SelfState reports Pull, Vault
and Break progress. NavGrid blocks Down shutters on change and offers rail
vault links; ChaseTactics makes chased survivor bots pull, vault and run to
rails and lets the killer bot break a shutter when the detour exceeds 8 m.

Also moves three rails off long-standing test lanes (SHARED_CHANGED), teaches
the harness planner to avoid rails, and exempts the idle-human-only wait from
the stall invariant.
