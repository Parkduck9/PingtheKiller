U4k-buildings: close the gaps in the city art

Wall boxes that share an edge now form one building group, computed from
FixedMap geometry. Every interior box gets an upper: generated stepped
wings (2-3 storeys) whose Setback applies only to the group outline, so
shared edges no longer open a slot, and fitted City 03 houses on the five
single-box buildings. Neighbouring groups get different colours, facades
and models. The skyline gets a continuous low terrace filling the strip
behind the border walls, shoulder-to-shoulder houses and generated filler
blocks instead of gaps.

Adds WorldArtCapture (batch PNG renders and triangle counts), the
WorldArtBuildingTests EditMode suite, and the before/after screens.
Walls + backdrop: 1,712,674 -> 1,570,550 triangles, 69 -> 127 renderers.
