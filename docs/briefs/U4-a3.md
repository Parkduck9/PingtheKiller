## The different approach
1. **You verify by pixels, not by reading.** You may (and must) run Unity batch builds and the built player yourself and look at the screenshots (Read tool on the PNG). Iterate until the screenshots are right. Never answer or click any Windows security/firewall dialog; loopback connections work without it.
2. **Dev capture mode in the client** (so lobby/play can be captured without keystrokes — external SendKeys does not reach the player window). A pure `DevArgs` parser (testable) over `Environment.GetCommandLineArgs()`, inert when no flag is present:
   - `-ptk-join <ip:port>` auto-join on start; `-ptk-bots <n>` and `-ptk-role killer|survivor|random` sent by the host after the first LobbyState; `-ptk-start` host sends StartMatch once the lobby has ≥ 2 participants;
   - `-ptk-hold-w <seconds>` scripted forward input during play from the 2nd second (goes through `MovementInput`, so the 30 Hz rule still applies);
   - `-ptk-shots <dir>` saves `ScreenCapture.CaptureScreenshot` PNGs: `menu.png` (1 s after start, before joining — capture the menu first when `-ptk-join` is also set), `lobby.png` (1 s after first LobbyState), `play-1s.png`, `play-4s.png` (after MatchStart);
   - `-ptk-quit-after <seconds>` then `Application.Quit()`.
   Unit-test the parser (valid/invalid/missing values) in `Client/Assets/Tests/EditMode/`.
3. **Layout rebuilt for deterministic text sizing.** Fix the cause, not the symptom: single-line labels must not wrap (`white-space: nowrap`), only the message line wraps, inside an explicit width with its height growing to fit; nothing may overlap or clip. You may move the screens to a UXML file in `Resources/` if that is cleaner. Keep everything from SLOP §8 (left-aligned list, one dominant primary, palette, 2 px radius, 4/8/12/16/24/32 spacing, Inter, 14/18/28 sizes) and all five button states.
